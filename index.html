Brotato Devs Tease “Endless Potato” Mode in Surprise Patch

Roguelite fans brace for wilder builds, more bullets, and one extremely overcaffeinated tuber.

By Lina “PatchNotes” Rivera Updated March 9, 2026 Early Access News Desk

The arena in Brotato just got a lot louder. A surprise balance patch, quietly pushed to test branches overnight, is experimenting with an experimental “Endless Potato” mode that removes the final wave and asks a simple question: how long can one extremely armed root vegetable survive?

According to an internal dev note spotted on the public changelog, the new mode leans harder into the chaotic buildcrafting that made Brotato so bingeable in the first place. Enemies scale past the usual cap, shops roll in stranger combinations, and defensive items pick up new synergies designed to keep glass‑cannon potatoes alive just long enough to make terrible decisions.

Highlights from the experimental patch
  • Endless waves with escalating enemy modifiers and rare “calm” rounds to catch your breath.
  • Prototype relics that alter the arena rules mid‑run, like double dashes or rotating hazard rings.
  • Reworked melee trees so brawling Brotatos scale better into the late game without becoming invincible.
  • A new “Commentator” announcer voice option that roasts your build choices between rounds.
  • Opt‑in challenge toggles so players can stack mutators instead of picking a single difficulty preset.

The developers stress that everything in this experimental patch is subject to change, and that leaderboards for the mode may remain separate or purely local. Still, it is a clear signal that Brotato’s endgame is evolving past simple wave clears and into a more sandbox‑style power fantasy for players who have memorized every weapon pairing on the roster.

If the tests go well, the studio hints that Endless Potato could become a permanent alternate queue, complete with cosmetic unlocks for particularly cursed builds that somehow refuse to die. Until then, veterans might want to dust off their favorite potatoes and start theorycrafting new paths to absurdity.

Editor’s note: This is an early look at an experimental patch. Details, tuning values, and even feature names may change before they are rolled into the main public branch.
