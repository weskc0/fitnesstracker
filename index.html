Brotato Devs Pilot “Endless Potato” Test Patch

A limited preview build teases longer runs, stranger modifiers, and new ways to push your favorite potato to the breaking point.

By Lina “PatchNotes” Rivera March 9, 2026 Indie Action Desk

Brotato’s arena has always been about barely controlled chaos, but the latest experimental patch leans into that idea even harder. A new “Endless Potato” test branch removes the traditional end point and simply asks how far your build can go before the incoming waves inevitably catch up.

In the preview build, enemies continue scaling far past the current cap, picking up new behavior tags that force players to move with more intention. Shops roll riskier stat bundles, defensive items receive slight boosts, and the pacing introduces occasional “calm” waves meant to give just enough breathing room to fix a bad decision or double down on a cursed one.

What players are seeing in the test build
  • Endless waves with scaling difficulty and occasional low‑intensity rounds to reset your nerves.
  • Prototype relics that bend the rules, like inverted healing or damage that ramps with dodge streaks.
  • Re‑tuned melee paths aimed at making close‑range potatoes less punishing deep into a run.
  • Optional challenge toggles that can be stacked, rather than a single static difficulty preset.
  • A separate experimental progression track so main‑branch saves stay untouched while feedback rolls in.

The developers say the mode is still firmly in the experimental stage: numbers are loose, relics are weird, and some ideas may be quietly retired before the patch graduates. Still, it is a clear sign that Brotato’s long‑term goal is less about ticking off a checklist and more about giving dedicated players a sandbox to stress‑test their strangest builds.

For now, the test remains opt‑in on a separate branch with no confirmed release window. That has not stopped theorycrafters from sketching out new build paths and arguing over which potatoes are secretly built for an endless arena instead of a neat, tidy victory screen.

Note: All details here are based on a preview build. Balance values, relic behavior, and even mode names may change before any wider rollout.
